📌 Information

The Booth Level Officer (BLO), in consultation with the Booth Level Agents (BLAs), has prepared the list of electors whose Enumeration Forms have not been received.

Aggrieved electors may submit Form-6 together with the prescribed Declaration Form and supporting documents during the Claims & Objections period from 31-07-2026 to 10-09-2026.

BLO–BLA Minutes are mapped with the Old Part Number. To locate an elector in the Minutes, kindly refer to the Old Part Number displayed in the ASDD list.
